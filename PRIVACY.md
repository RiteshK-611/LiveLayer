# Privacy Policy for LiveLayer

**Last Updated: December 7, 2025**

## Introduction

LiveLayer ("we", "our", or "the app") is a desktop wallpaper management application developed by Ritesh Kokam / Origo Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how LiveLayer handles user data.

## Data Collection

**LiveLayer does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.** The app operates entirely offline and locally on your device.

### What We Do NOT Collect:
- Personal identification information
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Browsing history
- Any data transmitted to external servers

### Local Data Storage

LiveLayer stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

| Data Type | Purpose | Storage Location |
|-----------|---------|------------------|
| Wallpaper file paths | To remember your selected wallpapers | Local app data folder |
| App preferences | To restore your settings (widget position, fonts, colors) | Local app data folder |
| Wallpaper list | To manage your wallpaper collection | Local app data folder |

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

## Third-Party Services

LiveLayer does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

## Data Security

Since all data remains on your local device, your information is protected by your operating system's built-in security measures. We recommend keeping your operating system updated and using appropriate security software.

## Children's Privacy

LiveLayer does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

## Your Rights

Since LiveLayer does not collect any personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, modify, or delete. All app data is stored locally and can be removed by uninstalling the application.

**Summary**: LiveLayer is a privacy-friendly application that operates entirely offline. No data is collected, transmitted, or shared with anyone.
